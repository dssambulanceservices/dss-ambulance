Our Purpose

Reliable Ambulance Services in Delhi NCR, Local & Outstation

DSS Ambulance Service provides dependable medical transportation services for patients requiring safe and timely travel to hospitals, clinics, homes, and other medical facilities.

We provide Normal Ambulance, Ventilator Ambulance, and Mortuary Ambulance Services, along with local and outstation medical transportation. Our services are available across Delhi NCR and for longer-distance journeys. We focus on providing a respectful, comfortable, and well-organised transportation experience for patients and their accompanying family members.
