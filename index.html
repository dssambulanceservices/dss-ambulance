Operational Coverage

Our Service Area

Based in Mayur Vihar Phase 3, Delhi, we cover the following areas and outstation routes:

  • Delhi NCR Coverage: East Delhi, South Delhi, Central Delhi, West Delhi, North Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, and Gurugram.
  • Outstation Routes: Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Punjab, Uttarakhand, and longer journeys across North India.
  • Major Hospitals: AIIMS, Safdarjung, Max, Fortis, Apollo, Jaypee, Yashoda, Kailash, and all local nursing homes.
