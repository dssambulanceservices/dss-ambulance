Ventilator Ambulance Service

For patients who require ventilator-supported medical transportation and appropriate medical assistance during their journey.

Equipped like an intensive care unit (ICU), this ambulance is tailored for critically ill patients who need continuous invasive/non-invasive ventilation, uninterrupted oxygen therapy, cardiac rhythm monitoring, and skilled paramedic attention throughout the transit.

  • Hospital-grade portable ventilators and multiparameter cardiac monitors

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Ambulance Services

Everything you need to know about emergency dispatch, ventilator facilities, mortuary hearse vans, and booking in Delhi NCR.

What types of ambulances are available with DSS Ambulance Service?

We maintain a versatile fleet including Normal Ambulances for routine patient checkups, dialysis and hospital discharges, Ventilator & ICU Ambulances equipped with advanced life-support ventilators, cardiac monitors, infusion pumps and paramedics, and Mortuary Ambulances with hygienic dead body freezer boxes for solemn transit.

How quickly can an ambulance reach my location in Delhi NCR?

Our primary dispatch station is at Mayur Vihar Phase 3, Delhi, with quick connectivity to Noida, East Delhi, South Delhi, and Ghaziabad. We immediately assign the nearest available standby vehicle to minimize response time.

Do you provide outstation patient and mortuary transfer?

Yes, we provide 24/7 outstation medical transportation from Delhi NCR to all major cities in Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow, Kanpur, Agra, Meerut, Bareilly), Haryana, Rajasthan, Punjab, Uttarakhand, and Bihar with trained drivers and medical attendants.

How do I book an ambulance immediately?

In emergencies, calling our direct 24/7 helplines 7840089777 or 7678514315 is the fastest way. You can also send patient details or live pickup location via WhatsApp at 7840089777.
